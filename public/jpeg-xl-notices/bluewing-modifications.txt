Bluewing modifications to libjxl 0.12.0
Pinned source: a7a9c787341cf703dede03c2009fa460cae5e5df

codecs/jpeg-xl/libjxl-memory.patch changes encoder buffer lifetimes.
codecs/jpeg-xl/libjxl-dc.patch appends a private bounded DC exporter to
lib/jxl/decode.cc. It reads decoded DC without flushing the full frame,
uses OpsinToLinear plus the default CMS for linear-sRGB to sRGB conversion,
and packs a reduced opaque RGBA8 snapshot. Unsupported images return zero.
Decoder continuation and the full-resolution output path are unchanged.

Source patches, facade, build instructions, and validation are in
codecs/jpeg-xl. Upstream copyright, license, and patent notices accompany
this file. Emscripten 4.0.23 builds the single-thread SIMD WASM assets.
